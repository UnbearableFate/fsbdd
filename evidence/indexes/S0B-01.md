- MPI places one role per node and carries no application data.
- Payload is written and fsynced before the small visibility record; records
  use same-directory temp files, `os.replace`, and directory fsync.
- Reader verifies run/peer identity, byte count and SHA-256 before an atomic
  acknowledgement.
