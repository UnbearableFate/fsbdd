sequence/base frontiers, selected proposal and base facts, float32 weights,

payload. Parameters, that envelope, version, outer-update count, and retained
base are announced by one fixed per-fragment current-record replacement.
There is no independent latest parameter, optimizer, frontier, or global-head
record.

All 16 fault cases cover the four storage boundaries four times. The 12
pre-replace interruptions expose the byte-identical old authority and
frontiers, retain proposal eligibility, and publish once on replay. The four
post-replace interruptions expose the complete new authority and exact retry
returns it without version or frontier advancement. The analyzer independently
binds request parameters, outer bytes, previous authority, selected facts,
policy, update identity, and complete frontier transition to every result.

minimum 378 per reader. Every reader sees both fragments at version 0 and
version 20, with all observed `(fragment, version, fingerprint)` tuples drawn
from the authoritative publication trace and no reader error. The late-arrival
fixture keeps the frozen two-proposal selection unchanged, leaves the late
learner unconsumed, rejects its old-base proposal, and selects its refreshed
proposal at the next version. Exact duplicate retry is idempotent. The
frontier remains four fixed entries at a synthetic sequence ordinal 10,000.
