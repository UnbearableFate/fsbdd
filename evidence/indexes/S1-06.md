wrong boundary, emits an unsafe event, reports a mean of rank-local rates,

## GREEN and hardening

`LearnerRuntime` executes one complete local HF causal LM with an exact local
optimizer, constant local-step scheduler, complete fragment partition, and an
owned RNG stream. Gradient accumulation sums each HF mean loss by its exact
shifted, non-padding target count and divides gradients once by the total
targets. Padding labels are normalized without mutating caller batches.
Progress changes only after backward, finite-gradient clipping,
`optimizer.step`, `scheduler.step`, and `zero_grad` form a safe boundary.

The immutable GPT-2/WikiText materialization contains 4,718 packed-512 blocks
and reproduces token-stream SHA-256 `b5b77d97...dc13`. The Pythia/FineWeb smoke
prefix contains 160 packed-512 blocks and reproduces `585a3477...4b0`. Its
frozen long-run mode selects `sample/10BT/*.parquet` in bytewise
lexicographic order and requires at least 1,000,000,000 packed input tokens.

Formal tiny, GPT-2 small, and Pythia-160m runs each complete ten optimizer
steps. GPT-2 and Pythia each process 20,480 input and 20,440 loss-bearing target
tokens. Their token-weighted losses are 3.7925769 and 4.2877489; their observed
parameter-update norms are 0.9888175 and 0.9901322. Each fragment ends with ten
local steps and the full per-profile input-token total. Throughput is computed
separately as total profile input tokens divided by its own
training-start-to-final-boundary interval; no combined learner rate is
synthesized. All 140 Stage 1 tests pass.
